Sprint Ceremonies (In Order)
1. Sprint PlanningCollaborative event where the Scrum Team plans work for the current sprint. Defines the Sprint Goal and selects backlog items.
2. Daily Standup15-minute timebox. Team reviews progress, declares intentions, highlights obstacles. Also called Daily Scrum.
3. Backlog RefinementOngoing activity (not an official Scrum event). Backlog is elaborated and re-prioritized. ~1 hour/week goal.
4. Sprint ReviewDemo of completed work to PO and stakeholders. Get feedback, accept/decline stories. Also called "Demo".
5. Sprint RetrospectiveTeam inspects process - what worked, what didn't. Creates improvement items for next sprint. INTERNAL team focus.
Key Distinctions
Review vs Retrospective:
• Review = PRODUCT focus (demo to stakeholders)
• Retrospective = PROCESS focus (team only)

Who runs standups?
• The TEAM runs standups (self-organizing)
• Scrum Master may facilitate but doesn't lead
• NOT a status meeting for management!

📋 PM as Servant Leader in Agile
DO: Remove impediments, facilitate, coach, protect the team, enable collaboration

DON'T: Assign tasks, collect status, make decisions for team, bypass PO on scope
⚠️ Common Exam Traps
• PM should NOT "run" standups as status meetings
• PM should NOT assign tasks (team self-organizes)
• PM should NOT add scope mid-sprint (that's PO)
• PM SHOULD remove impediments surfaced at standup
• PM SHOULD ensure stakeholders attend Sprint Review
• PM SHOULD protect Retrospective from outsiders
Scrum Artifacts
Product BacklogOrdered list of all requirements. Owned by Product Owner.
Sprint BacklogItems selected for current sprint + plan to deliver increment.
IncrementSum of all completed items - must be "Done" and usable.
Scrum Roles
Product OwnerMaximizes product value. Manages backlog. One person (not committee).
Scrum MasterServant leader. Removes impediments. Coaches team. Facilitates events.
Development TeamCross-functional, self-organizing. 3-9 members. Delivers increment.
