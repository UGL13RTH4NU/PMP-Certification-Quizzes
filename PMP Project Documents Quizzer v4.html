33 Project Documents (PMBOK 6)
Project documents support the project management plan and provide detailed information throughout the project lifecycle. They differ from the plan components as they're supporting materials rather than guiding elements.
Process Groups Legend
Initiating Planning Executing Monitoring Closing
💡 Study Tips
• Focus on which process creates each document
• Understand the purpose of each document
• Note documents that are logs vs. registers
• Pay attention to resource-related documents
• Remember quality and risk documents
